About Me

My Goal

My personal goal is to help as many people as possible with their technology needs. I am passionate about the intersection of human connection and technical efficiency.

My Philosophy

I love solving problems—including the ones that don’t strictly "need" to be solved. My personal slogan is: “If it isn’t broke, do fix it.” I believe in continuous improvement and optimization rather than just maintenance.

Persistence & Dedication

I pride myself on my persistence. Whether it's staying late at work to ensure plant orders are completed or spending hours at home troubleshooting a custom operating system on an old Google Pixel, I see my projects through to the end. I’ve even reinstalled Windows multiple times just to explore different versions and find the perfect configuration.

My Personal Computer Fleet

I maintain a fleet of six computers: a Dell Inspiron that I plan to run Slax Linux on, a 2011 Intel Mac running Debian, an M1 Mac running macOS 27, an HP laptop I received for free from Fishbeck, another HP laptop I received for free from a couple of friends, and an Intel NUC running Alpine Linux.

On my Windows computer, I also run four virtual machines: Windows 11, macOS 26 (Tahoe), Ubuntu, and Kali Linux. Virtual machines let me try different operating systems and setups on one physical computer.

Keeping this collection useful is an ongoing project. I update software, upgrade systems, maintain their configurations, and work through problems as they come up. It gives me hands-on practice caring for a mix of older and newer hardware across Windows, macOS, and Linux.

Linux & Everyday Automation

I enjoy tinkering with Linux, keeping older computers useful, and finding small ways to make everyday technology easier to use. My Maintenance & Experiments page records the remote-access fixes, Windows permission troubleshooting, USB setups, and local AI experiments along the way.

I also love creating email rules to keep a highly organized inbox. Working with AI, I built a Google Apps Script that moves Gmail spam threads to Trash automatically every 30 minutes. The project and code snippet show how a small automation can take care of a repetitive task.

Fun Facts & Everyday Organization

I love finding practical tricks to keep everyday technology tidy. On my phone, I organize multiple numbers using two dedicated consolidated contacts: one contact combines 20+ two-factor verification code numbers and automated SMS shortcodes into a single message thread so one-time security codes don't clutter my texts, and another contact bundles 10+ numbers that are either spam or scams so nuisance calls and texts can be easily identified, silenced, and managed in one place.

Community & Communication

I enjoy using my public speaking skills to share my passions. From serving on the Student Council in Middle School to helping my grandma stop a flood of spam notifications, I am never afraid to step in and communicate technical solutions clearly and effectively.

My work at the GVSU IT Service Desk has strengthened my phone communication skills, especially explaining technical issues clearly and helping callers work through troubleshooting steps.

Experimenting with AI & Computing

I enjoy working with a variety of AI chatbots to explore ideas and solve problems. I also experiment with AI image generation on my 2011 Intel Mac, sometimes using around 48 GB of swap, and with local AI music generation and model training through ACE-Step. I describe that ongoing work on my projects page.

Computing for Research & Experimentation

I contribute spare computing power to Folding@Home, helping researchers simulate how proteins behave and study diseases. Through BananoMiner, that work also earns small BANANO rewards. Getting back into folding gave me a chance to trace payout records, restore my existing wallet, and learn how pending transfers are received.

I also experiment with Monero mining to learn about hardware performance, resource use, and mining pools. It is a hands-on computing experiment I enjoy maintaining across my computer fleet.
