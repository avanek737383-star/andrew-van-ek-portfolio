Module 02

AI Word Predictions & Job Stereotypes

Language models predict the next word in a sentence based on millions of public articles and forum posts. If past articles usually talked about doctors as men or nurses as women, the AI repeats that exact same assumption. Pick a sentence below to see which pronoun the model expects next.

⚡

Why this happens in plain English:

The AI isn't thinking for itself—it is simply playing a high-speed guessing game of 'what word usually comes next?' If the older books and websites it studied wrote 'he' after doctor and 'she' after nurse, it guesses those words every time unless engineers deliberately teach it to stay neutral.

Module 03

The Accuracy Trap: Why 95% Can Still Be Completely Broken

Think of a security guard watching 1,000 people enter a stadium, where only 2 people are carrying fake tickets. If the guard falls asleep and waves everyone through without checking a single ticket, they were '99.8% accurate' on paper—yet they failed at the one job they were hired to do. That's the Accuracy Trap.

Group Balance in the Data: 90% Majority / 10% Minority

How to Fix It:

90.0% Overall Accuracy (Sounds great on paper)
98.0% Majority Catch Rate (Passes easily)
18.0% Minority Catch Rate (Completely left behind!)
0.80 Opportunity Gap (Target: under 0.05)
💡

The Big Takeaway: When an AI is tested on skewed data, a high accuracy score is often just a lazy guess. Adjusting the pass line or using SMOTE balancing forces the model to actually look at qualifications instead of taking the easy way out.

Module 04

The 3 Ways Engineers Fix Algorithmic Bias

You don't fix bias by hoping the computer is nice—you fix it by engineering checks before, during, and after the model makes decisions.

Stage What It Means Everyday Real-World Comparison The Measurement Rule
Before Training (Pre-Processing) Cleaning the data and balancing the groups before teaching the computer Making sure a cookbook has healthy recipes from all over the world instead of 50 pages of just cupcakes Passing Rate Ratio (DIR ≥ 0.80)
During Training (In-Processing) Penalizing the AI whenever it relies on shortcuts like names, gender, or age Telling a music judge they get docked points if they look at who is playing instead of listening to the song Demographic Parity (≤ 0.05 gap)
After Training (Post-Processing) Adjusting the pass/fail line so equally qualified people get fair results Calibrating sports tryout cutoffs so honest players aren't eliminated by bad weather on their tryout day Equal Opportunity Gap (≤ 0.05)
