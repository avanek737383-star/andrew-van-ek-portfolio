Module 02

LLM Next-Token Probability & Stereotype Explorer

Autoregressive Large Language Models predict next-token distributions based on conditional probabilities learned from massive Internet scrapes (Common Crawl, Reddit, C4). When training corpora reflect occupational stereotypes, the model's logits strongly diverge based on demographic pronouns.

⚡

Why this happens in Foundation Models:

In web-scraped training datasets, occupational titles appear co-located with gendered pronouns in asymmetrical proportions. Transformer self-attention heads learn these associative vectors as high-confidence statistical priors. Without RLHF steering, DPO (Direct Preference Optimization), or activation steering, standard greedily sampled generation mirrors these legacy occupational imbalances.

Module 03

The Accuracy Paradox & Class Imbalance Simulator

In high-stakes fraud detection, cybersecurity malware scanning, and healthcare diagnostics, minority groups or anomalous classes often represent less than 5% of the total dataset. A naive classifier can achieve a stellar 95%+ overall accuracy score simply by predicting "Negative" for every single sample—completely failing the protected subgroup.

Class Distribution Imbalance: 90% Majority / 10% Minority

Remediation Method:

Notice how overall model accuracy remains misleadingly high, while the model is failing to identify the protected minority class! Without fairness metrics (True Positive Rate Parity), stakeholders are blinded by aggregate statistics.

Framework

Solutions Analyst AI Fairness & Governance Matrix

Addressing bias requires deliberate engineering checkpoints throughout the machine learning development lifecycle (MLDLC). Here is the mitigation framework I follow across enterprise solution architectures:

Stage Diagnostic Technique Engineering Intervention Key Metric / Standard
Pre-Processing Representation Auditing & Correlation Mapping Synthetic re-sampling (SMOTE), re-weighing, and proxy attribute disentanglement Disparate Impact Ratio (DIR ≥ 0.80)
In-Processing Adversarial Debiasing & Fairness Loss Regularization Add a discriminator gradient penalty that penalizes predicting protected attributes from latent features Demographic Parity Difference (ΔDP ≤ 0.05)
Post-Processing ROC Curve Calibration & Equalized Odds Tuning Calibrate separate classification thresholds per group to align True Positive and False Positive rates Equal Opportunity Difference (ΔEOD ≤ 0.05)
Continuous Monitoring Embedding Drift & Logit Telemetry Automated drift detection alerts when inference inputs deviate from certified distribution baselines Population Stability Index (PSI < 0.10)
