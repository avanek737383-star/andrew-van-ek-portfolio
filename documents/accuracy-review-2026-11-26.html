What this review establishes

This review checked the sixteen reported findings against page code, recovered experiment artifacts, controlled-host configuration, and targeted tests. Corrections distinguish synthetic examples, reproduced measurements, illustrative graphics, historical observations, and unverified deployment claims.

It is not an independent certification of every historical statement in the portfolio. Older project and maintenance notes contain owner-reported observations and session-specific figures; an unlinked benchmark, hardware lifetime, security assurance, or clinical interpretation should not be treated as independently reproduced evidence.

The transparent training experiment

One controlled candidate uses 20,000 training rows, 5,000 validation rows, and 15,000 final test rows from separate seeds. Validation loss selected checkpoint 36; the run stopped at epoch 44. The final test set was evaluated after that selection. A deterministic rerun after artifact-export changes reproduced the same counts.

On the final synthetic mixture: original model 54.47%, candidate 56.19%, always-switch baseline 55.89%. Candidate accuracy is 66.25% on invented alternation, 49.16% on mild persistence, and 49.82% on fair outcomes. The observed improvement is not a general or statistically established real-world advantage.
