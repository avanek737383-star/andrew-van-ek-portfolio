4. The Homelab Salvage Playbook & ITAM Architecture

Key technical practices and IT Asset Management protocols used to keep aging silicon running reliably 24/7 without budget or thermal degradation.

01

Phase-Change Thermal Engineering

Liquid and standard silicone pastes pump out under the thermal expansion cycles of 24/7 100% compute loads. Upgrading legacy laptops (such as the 2011 MacBook Pro) with Honeywell PTM7950 phase-change pads prevents dry-out and drops sustained core temperatures by 8-12°C indefinitely.

02

Stateless & Immutable Memory Runtimes

Mechanical drives and cheap consumer SSDs fail early under relentless logging. Deploying custom Slax Linux live USBs with RAM overlays (like the Dell Inspiron tower) ensures that the operating system runs purely in memory, making nodes immune to drive failures and corrupt writes.

03

Fire-Safe Battery Elimination

Lithium-ion pouch batteries in retired mobile devices and laptops will swell when held at 100% state-of-charge on constant AC power. Removing internal batteries and hard-wiring regulated 5V/12V DC lines (or replacing with dummy safety resistors) guarantees safe unmonitored homelab operations.

04

GLPI IT Asset Management (ITAM)

Tracking 7 heterogeneous machines with distinct maintenance windows requires disciplined asset lifecycle tracking. Integrating hardware specs, service dates, thermal profiles, and IP/MAC allocations into a centralized GLPI asset management schema ensures zero undocumented single-points-of-failure.
