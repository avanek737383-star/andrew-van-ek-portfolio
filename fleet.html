2. The Homelab Salvage Playbook & ITAM Architecture

Practices used in this particular fleet. Hardware condition and results vary; these notes are not lifetime, temperature, or unattended-safety guarantees.

01

Phase-Change Thermal Engineering

Thermal-interface changes can affect sustained temperatures, but results depend on the device, mounting, airflow, workload, and ambient conditions. The maintenance notes describe PTM7950 experiments; they do not establish a universal 8–12 °C improvement or an indefinite lifetime.

02

Stateless & Immutable Memory Runtimes

A Slax RAM overlay can reduce writes to storage and keep runtime changes in memory. Boot media, saved persistence, and power can still fail. Unsaved RAM-backed changes can be lost; this setup is not immunity to corruption or a substitute for backups.

03

Battery Condition & Power Configuration

Battery aging and swelling depend on condition, design, temperature, and charging behavior. A battery-free configuration changes power requirements and removes only one possible failure source; it does not guarantee safe unattended operation. Follow the device manufacturer’s service guidance and check the equipment’s condition.

04

GLPI IT Asset Management (ITAM)

Tracking 9 heterogeneous devices across multiple shelves requires disciplined asset lifecycle tracking. Integrating hardware specs, service dates, thermal profiles, and IP/MAC allocations into a centralized GLPI asset management schema helps document dependencies and possible single points of failure.
