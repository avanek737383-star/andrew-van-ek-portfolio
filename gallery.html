Visual Gallery & Security Workbench

Real-world payload detonations, forensics artifacts, retro virtualization & distributed compute fleet telemetry.

Not every experiment belongs inside a polished project case study or an operational changelog. This visual workbench captures hands-on explorations across my physical and virtual lab environments—from live malware detonations safely contained inside Windows Sandbox to vintage operating system revivals, digital forensics coursework, and 24/7 distributed compute nodes.

Studying active threats firsthand was an eye-opening lesson in defensive discipline: watching aggressive payloads completely shatter Windows Explorer, hijack desktop input hooks, and scramble files in real time cemented the paramount importance of strict testing safety. When handled with proper isolation protocols, dissecting software behavior is both fascinating and essential for building resilient systems.

🛡️
Safety & Containment Protocol: All volatile malware samples and suspicious binaries featured below were detonated exclusively within Windows Sandbox—an ephemeral, Hyper-V-isolated disposable container. No host network shares or persistence existed. Every session was completely incinerated upon closing, ensuring zero risk to physical hardware or production networks.
