Visual Gallery & Security Workbench

Real-world payload detonations, file integrity destruction, and threat analysis studied safely in disposable sandboxes.

Not every experiment belongs inside a polished project case study or a dry maintenance log. This visual gallery captures hands-on explorations at my security workbench—from live ransomware samples downloaded from the Da2dalus malware repository to deceptive screen lockers and recursive fork-bombs.

Studying active threats firsthand was an eye-opening lesson in defensive engineering: watching aggressive payloads completely break Windows Explorer, seize desktop focus, and scramble files in real time cemented the paramount importance of strict testing discipline. When handled with proper isolation protocols, dissecting malware is both fascinating and essential for building robust enterprise defenses.

🛡️
Safety & Isolation Protocol: All samples featured below were analyzed exclusively within Windows Sandbox—an ephemeral, Hyper-V-isolated disposable container. No host network shares or persistence existed. Every session was completely incinerated upon closing, ensuring zero risk to the physical workstation or internal network.
