      3D Planetary Radar Architecture & Physical Internet Telemetry

      While the internet is often abstracted as an ephemeral "cloud", in physical reality over 95% of international data transit relies on dense glass fibers traversing extreme ocean trenches. This planetary radar maps the physical layer of the global web:

      Subsea Optical Fiber Topologies

      Ingests TeleGeography's geospatial datasets, rendering multi-line vector paths for over 400 active international subsea cable systems. Dashed particle shaders animate kinetic photon pulses simulating light propagation across Transatlantic, Transpacific, and Arctic seabed corridors.

      Coastal Landing Stations & Street View

      Pinpoints 1,900+ coastal cable landing stations where armored wet marine cables transition into terrestrial beach manholes. Selecting any station executes a smooth camera swoop and surfaces direct one-click links to Google Street View and Mapillary panoramas.

      Tier 1 Global Peering & Backhaul

      Maps core international Internet Exchange Points (including Equinix Ashburn, DE-CIX Frankfurt, AMS-IX Amsterdam, and LINX London), tracing high-capacity optical transit arcs between subsea landings and continental backbones.
