      How to Navigate the 3D Constellation

      • Orbit & Rotate: Click and drag anywhere in open space to rotate the camera around the 3D universe.
      • Zoom & Pan: Scroll your mouse wheel or pinch on touchscreens to zoom. Right-click and drag (or two-finger drag) to pan across clusters.
      • Inspect & Fly-To: Click any node to fly the camera into it, highlight its connections, and open the architectural telemetry drawer.
      • Search: Type in the search box to highlight matching projects and instantly jump to them.
      • Zero Tailscale Dependency: The entire constellation executes client-side in WebGL, rendering natively on mobile and desktop browsers worldwide.
