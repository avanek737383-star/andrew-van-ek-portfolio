Data Ethics & Transparency

Privacy Policy

How this website and my homelab services handle network traffic, visitor telemetry, and AI conversations.

1. Zero Raw IP Address Storage

✓ Zero Raw IP Retention In accordance with my privacy principles, zero raw IP addresses or personally identifiable coordinates are ever logged, stored in databases, or published.

When you visit the site, network edge requests pass through Cloudflare proxy nodes to protect my self-hosted homelab. The homepage displays coarse regional telemetry (such as approximate country, region, or city) purely to show aggregate global visitor trends. This data is derived dynamically on the edge without capturing or retaining your physical street address or exact location.

2. Local AI & Data Sovereignty (Nemo)

My on-premise AI assistant (Nemo) runs locally on a dedicated NVIDIA RTX GPU host connected via a private Tailscale mesh network through an Alpine Linux Intel NUC gateway:

  • No Third-Party Brokers: Prompts sent to Nemo are processed on my own private hardware. They are never sent to commercial data aggregators, advertising brokers, or public model training pipelines.
  • Temporary Abuse Review: Transient rate-limiting checks (5 messages/minute) are enforced in memory to protect local hardware from automated denial-of-service loops.
  • No Account Tracking: You do not need to sign in or provide an email to chat with Nemo.

3. Dual-Brain Cloud Assistant (Google Cloud Run)

The companion assistant at AI Assistant runs on Google Cloud Run utilizing Vertex AI Agent Builder. Queries are strictly used to search my grounded knowledge repositories (649 GVSU IT service-desk articles and ~977 OneDrive CIS class documents). Zero personal search profiles are constructed from your inquiries.

4. Client-Side Browser Persistence (localStorage)

To provide responsive interactive tools without server-side tracking, certain preferences and benchmarks are stored exclusively inside your own browser using standard localStorage:

  • Page Load Speed Tachometer: Your fastest page render record (milliseconds) and personal visit counter stay strictly in your browser.
  • Nemo Chat History: Your active conversation is retained on your device so you can navigate across portfolio pages without losing answers. You can clear this anytime by clicking the Clear button in the chat header.
  • Weather Units: Temperature scale preferences (°F vs. °C) are remembered locally.

None of this local data is ever transmitted back to an external tracking database.

5. Zero Third-Party Advertising Trackers

This website contains zero marketing cookies, zero Facebook Pixels, and zero commercial tracking beacons. I do not run banner advertisements or monetize visitor data in any way.

6. Contact Information

If you have any questions about this policy or would like to discuss homelab security and privacy practices, feel free to reach out directly via the Contact page or email avanek737383@gmail.com.
