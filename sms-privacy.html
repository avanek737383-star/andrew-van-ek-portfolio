Privacy Policy

Andrew Van Ek - Personal AI SMS

Privacy Policy

Effective November 26, 2026. This policy covers the private AI SMS assistant and optional website-update texts operated by Andrew Van Ek.

What is collected

When you text the service, Twilio and your carrier process your phone number, message, delivery information, and any attachment. The local gateway records message identifiers, sender and recipient numbers, timestamps, delivery status, and error codes. For approved AI users, it also records text questions and replies to provide conversation context. Messages from other numbers are not passed to the AI or stored as conversation content. Attachments are not analyzed in the current SMS version.

Subscriptions and consent

Sending UPDATES records your phone number, the time and message identifier of your consent, and your subscription status. STOP disables your website-update subscription. Consent and opt-out records may be retained to honor your choices, prevent unwanted messages, and resolve delivery problems. No subscriber list is displayed publicly.

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Processing and service providers

Twilio, telecommunications carriers, and network providers process messages to deliver the service. AI responses are generated on Andrew's local hardware. Conversation content is not sent to an external AI model provider by this gateway. SMS is not end-to-end encrypted; avoid sending passwords or sensitive information.

Local retention and controls

AI context uses up to six recent exchanges within a 24-hour window. RESET clears the approved sender's conversation context. Local message and delivery diagnostic records are pruned after seven days as new messages arrive; RESET does not erase those diagnostic records. Twilio and carriers maintain separate records under their own policies.

Contact and requests

For help or a request about your local records, email avanek737383@gmail.com. The service may verify that you control the number before acting on a request. You can stop website-update messages at any time by texting STOP.
